Game log

    How to play
    • At the start of every round each player puts $1 in the pot.
    • On your turn you roll six dice. After every roll you must keep at least one die (tap to pick), then roll the rest. Kept dice stay kept.
    • Keep going until all six dice are kept.
    • To qualify you need a 1 and a 4 among your kept dice. Your score is the total of the other four dice, so the best possible hand is 1, 4, 6, 6, 6, 6 = 24.
    • No 1 or no 4 means you don't qualify.
    • If a die rolls off the table, you put another $1 in the pot and that roll doesn't count. Roll again.
    • High score takes the pot. A tie or nobody qualifying means the pot carries over to the next round.
