Game log

    How to play
    • At the start of every round each player puts $1 in the pot.
    • On your turn you get up to 3 rolls of five dice.
    • You need a 6 (Ship), then a 5 (Captain), then a 4 (Crew) — in that order. A 5 doesn't count until you have your 6, and a 4 doesn't count until you have your 5. They lock automatically.
    • Once you have Ship, Captain & Crew, the other two dice are your cargo (their total is your score, 2–12). You can keep it or use any rolls you have left to re-roll the cargo.
    • No crew by the end of your third roll means you score nothing.
    • If a die rolls off the table, you put another $1 in the pot and that roll doesn't count — roll again.
    • Highest cargo takes the pot. A tie or nobody qualifying means the pot carries over to the next round.
